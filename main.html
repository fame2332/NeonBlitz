WAVES & CONTROLS

Survive increasingly difficult waves of enemy drones.
Each wave brings more enemies and greater challenges.
Power-ups appear randomly to help you survive!

WASD - Move
MOUSE - Aim
LEFT CLICK - Shoot
R - Reload
SPACE - Jump
SHIFT - Dash
ESC - Pause

AMMO & RELOAD

You start with 30 ammo.
Press R to reload when out of ammo.
Watch for Ammo Pack power-ups to replenish your ammo!
