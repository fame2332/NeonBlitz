Game Outline: "Neon Blitz"
Concept
Title: Neon Blitz
Genre: Fast-paced 3D FPS
Theme: Retro-futuristic neon arena with glowing enemies and vibrant visuals.
Objective: Survive waves of enemy drones in a compact arena, score points, and share your high score on social media.
Viral Appeal:
Simple controls for accessibility.
Eye-catching neon visuals and particle effects.
Shareable scoreboard with one-click tweet functionality.
Short, addictive gameplay sessions (2-3 minutes per round).
Core Mechanics
Player:
First-person perspective with a laser gun.
Movement: WASD keys for navigation, mouse to aim, left-click to shoot.
Health: 100 HP, depletes when hit by enemy projectiles.
Score: Earn points by destroying enemies (10 points per enemy).
Enemies:
Floating neon drones that spawn in waves.
Behavior: Slowly move toward the player and shoot projectiles at intervals.
Waves: Every 30 seconds, a new wave spawns with more drones (Wave 1: 5 drones, Wave 2: 8 drones, etc.).
Arena:
A compact, enclosed 3D space with neon-lit walls and obstacles (cubes) for cover.
Size: 50x50x50 units to keep action tight and intense.
Game Loop:
Start: Player spawns in the arena, and Wave 1 begins.
Progression: Survive waves, destroy enemies, and rack up points.
Game Over: When player HP reaches 0, display score and prompt to share or restart.
Controls:
WASD: Move forward, backward, strafe left, strafe right.
Mouse: Look/aim.
Left-click: Shoot laser (0.5-second cooldown).
Space: Jump (limited height for balance).
Viral Features:
Score Sharing: Button to tweet score with a hashtag (#NeonBlitz).
Visual Flair: Particle effects on enemy destruction, glowing trails for projectiles.
Audio: Simple looping background music and sound effects (pew-pew for shots, explosions for enemy deaths).
Technical Implementation
Framework: Three.js for 3D rendering, physics, and scene management.
Libraries:
Three.js (via CDN) for 3D graphics.
Cannon.js (via CDN) for basic physics (collisions, gravity).
Structure:
Single HTML file with embedded JavaScript for easy browser deployment.
Modular code for scene setup, player controls, enemy AI, and UI.
Performance:
Low-poly models (cubes, spheres) to ensure smooth performance in browsers.
Limit particle effects to maintain 60 FPS on average hardware.
Browser Compatibility:
Tested for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge.
Responsive canvas scaling for different screen sizes.
Visual and Audio Design
Visuals:
Neon color palette (pink, blue, purple) with black background.
Glowing materials (MeshBasicMaterial with emissive properties).
Particle effects for explosions and projectile trails.
Audio:
Background: Upbeat synthwave track (royalty-free, embedded as MP3).
Sound Effects: Laser shot (pew), enemy explosion (boom), hit feedback (ding).

Monetization/Shareability
No Monetization: Purely for fun and virality.
Shareability:
Tweet button: "I scored [score] in Neon Blitz! Beat me! #NeonBlitz [link]".
Short rounds encourage quick play and sharing.
Future Expansion:
Leaderboard (requires server, not implemented here).
Power-ups (e.g., speed boost, shield) for added depth.
Development Plan
Setup: Initialize Three.js scene, camera, renderer, and Cannon.js physics.
Player: Implement FPS controls using PointerLockControls, add shooting mechanic.
Enemies: Create drone AI with simple pathfinding and projectile spawning.
Arena: Build a static arena with walls and obstacles.
Game Loop: Manage waves, scoring, and game-over state.
Polish: Add particle effects, audio, and UI (score, health, game-over screen).
Share Feature: Implement tweet button with score.
